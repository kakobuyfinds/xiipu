Field note 01 / Sneakers and sizing

A sneaker shortlist starts with fit

For a Nike sneaker size comparison, begin with the exact model and the seller's measurement method. A size conversion table alone cannot account for differences in shape or construction.

Measure both feet while standing, using the same units as the chart. When reading a foot length vs insole length explanation, remember that these describe different things: one measures your foot; the other measures a component inside the shoe.

  • Check the variant: a sneaker colorway comparison should use the same model, lighting context and selected option.
  • Check the pair: a shoe stitching inspection should consider both shoes, visible seams and attachment points.
  • Check usability: request a shoe outsole photo check when tread, wear or visible damage is unclear.

Keep a listing on your shortlist only when you can explain why its measurements and intended use suit you.

Field note 02 / Clothing and QC

Read clothing photos as evidence

A useful hoodie chest measurement guide begins with a garment you already like. Lay it flat, measure across the chest and compare the same measurement on the listing. Confirm whether the chart shows flat width or full circumference.

For a T-shirt fabric weight comparison, use a stated specification when available; do not estimate fabric weight from an image. A jacket shoulder fit check should also consider whether the design uses set-in, dropped or raglan sleeves.

  • Bottoms: a pants inseam measurement check should include rise and waist; a shorts waist size guide should distinguish relaxed from stretched elastic.
  • Sportswear: for a jersey print inspection, look at placement, visible edges and the selected name or number.
  • Matching pieces: a matching set size comparison should check top and bottom measurements separately.
  • Personal items: complete an underwear fabric composition check and read the applicable return conditions before ordering.

For a warehouse QC photo checklist, request the specific angle or measurement that resolves your uncertainty. General photos may show visible condition, but they cannot establish comfort, long-term durability or authenticity.

A brand authenticity evidence check is separate from a visual QC review. Logos and packaging alone do not establish provenance.

Field note 03 / Shopping budget

Build a budget around the whole journey

A first haul budget checklist should keep confirmed charges separate from estimates. An attractive item price tells you little about the cost of getting the order to your address.

What to record before committing
Cost or condition What to verify
Item and local delivery Selected variant price, quantity and any domestic shipping charge.
Payment and conversion Checkout currency, effective exchange rate and any payment or service charge.
International freight Packed dimensions, weight, destination and the chosen route's charging method.
Destination charges Which charges are included, which may be payable later and what documentation is needed.
Returns Eligibility, deadline, return address and responsibility for delivery costs.

For parcel weight vs volumetric weight, check the selected carrier's formula and charging rules. A parcel consolidation cost comparison should use the proposed packed parcel rather than adding item weights alone.

A return policy before ordering check is equally useful: find out whether the deadline could expire while you are still reviewing the item. For an international shipping estimate checklist, save the destination, route, dimensions and estimate date together.

If a number is still an estimate, label it that way. Recheck the complete total when the parcel and shipping option are known.

Clear answers

Before you follow a find.

Understand what this guide provides, where its links lead and what you still need to verify.

What is a SuperBuy spreadsheet?

The term commonly describes a collection of product references used to research a potential buying-agent order. This page is an independent guide, not a live spreadsheet, checkout or official SuperBuy service.

Where do the links on this page go?

The category shortcuts open their named categories on cafans.com. Every other navigation link opens https://www.cafans.com. These links do not open a SuperBuy checkout.

Do QC photos prove authenticity?

No. QC photos can help document visible condition, dimensions and construction. They do not establish brand authorization or authenticity; review provenance and use an appropriate authentication service when needed.

How should I compare sizes?

Compare measurements in the same units and confirm how they were taken. For clothing, measure a garment that fits you well. For shoes, distinguish foot length, insole length and the seller's size recommendation.

Does an item price include international shipping?

Do not assume it does. Check the current listing and checkout, then account for domestic delivery, service or payment costs, international freight and any applicable destination charges.
